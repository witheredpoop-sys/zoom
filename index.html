Render · Nametags

Tags that read the fight back.

Nametags were rebuilt around a compact dark plate: name and totem-pop counter on the first row, distance on the right and a smoothed health bar along the bottom. Switch on the HP number, hands, armor and enchantment labels when you want the full read.

Totem pops Smoothed health Gear + enchants Tag scale
Render · Trails

Leave a line worth following.

Footsteps, expanding ground rings or a soft particle spray, stamped where a player walks and faded over a lifetime you set. It follows the menu accent by default, takes a custom color, and can stay on your own trail only.

Footsteps / Ring / Particles Accent color Self only
Render · Jump Circles

A neon ring for every takeoff.

The moment a player leaves the ground, a ring flashes and expands from their feet: bright core, quadratic growth and a soft glow bleeding out of both edges. Radius, duration, thickness and glow distance are all adjustable.

Jump ring Adjustable glow Theme color
Render · CS Knife

Your sword, with better manners.

Hold a sword and it becomes a butterfly knife, karambit or katana. Deploy and inspect animations, a glowing slash trail, blade sparkle and a real first-person arm — press F to inspect, hold it to keep spinning.

Butterfly / Karambit / Katana F to inspect Slash trail
Macro · Lunge

Dash on the spear. Keep the sword.

One key selects a Lunge spear, sends the jab and switches back before you notice. Your weapon stays in hand, nothing fires while a screen is open, and a cooldown, hunger gate and optional humanized switch-back keep it quiet.

One-key dash Keeps your slot Humanized

The interface

A menu that moves with you.

Open it with right shift. Search a module, flip a toggle, open a setting — every action has weight, sound and a clean exit. The menu is fast because it is built from the same primitives as the game, not a browser in a box.

  • Live search with keyboard-first navigation.
  • Six palettes, UI sounds and menu ambience.
  • Every module has real settings, not placeholder rows.
  • In-game HUD and click GUI share the same theme.
