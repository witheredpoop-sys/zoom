What happens after you pay

You get a key in the ticket. Open the Zoom launcher, sign in with the key, and the client is tied to your account. No separate download from a random mirror.

How do the plans work?

Monthly is $20 and renews until you cancel. Lifetime is a one-time $40 payment with no renewal. Both include every module and updates.

Do I need both versions?

There is only one version: Minecraft 1.21.11 on Fabric. Zoom does not have a 1.8.9 client and there is no plan to make one.

Questions before you buy?
